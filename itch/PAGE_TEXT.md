## Description

Click the `<>` button in the description editor, delete what is there and paste this:

```html
<h2>How high can a chicken go?</h2>
<p>Chicken Jump is an endless arcade jumper. Your chicken never stops bouncing, so all you have to do is steer it from platform to platform, up through day, sunset, night and into outer space. One bad jump and it's a long way down!</p>

<h2>What's waiting up there</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tricky platforms:</strong> some move, some crumble under your feet and clouds vanish after a single bounce.</li>
<li><strong>Monsters:</strong> shoot spiky balls and bats out of the sky, or stomp them from above.</li>
<li><strong>Limited ammo:</strong> you carry 5 shots and get one back every 3 jumps, so make them count.</li>
<li><strong>Screen wrap:</strong> run off one side and come back on the other.</li>
<li><strong>Rising difficulty:</strong> the higher you climb, the wider the gaps and the more dangerous the sky.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Power-ups</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Spring:</strong> launches you way up.</li>
<li><strong>Jetpack:</strong> rockets you upward, and nothing can touch you while it burns.</li>
<li><strong>Shield:</strong> absorbs one monster hit.</li>
<li><strong>Heart:</strong> an extra life that saves you from a hit or a fall. Hold up to 3.</li>
<li><strong>Bomb:</strong> wipes out every monster on screen. Hold up to 3.</li>
<li><strong>Triple shot:</strong> fires three shots at once for 10 seconds.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Controls</h2>
<table>
<tr><th>Action</th><th>Keyboard and mouse</th><th>Touchscreen</th></tr>
<tr><td>Move</td><td>Left/Right arrows or A/D</td><td>On-screen buttons</td></tr>
<tr><td>Shoot</td><td>Space, W, Up arrow or left click</td><td>On-screen button</td></tr>
<tr><td>Bomb</td><td>E, Down arrow or right click</td><td>On-screen button</td></tr>
</table>
<p>The game runs in your browser on desktop and mobile. There is also a Windows download.</p>

<h2>Global leaderboard</h2>
<p>Pick a name and see how your best score stacks up against other players.</p>

<h2>Languages</h2>
<p>English, Português, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Русский, 한국어, 中文. Change the language on the main menu.</p>

<h2>Privacy</h2>
<p>To run the leaderboard, the game sends your chosen name, your score and a random ID generated on your device to the <a href="https://trytalo.com">Talo</a> service.</p>

<p><em>Made with Godot Engine. Third-party licenses are included in LICENSES.txt.</em></p>
```
